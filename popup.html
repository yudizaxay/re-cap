re:cap

Before you record

re:cap records the audio of the tab you choose (and its video or your screen, if you select that) plus your microphone. Recordings are stored only in this browser.

To make a transcript, the audio is sent to the AI provider you pick in Settings (Groq, OpenAI, Google Gemini or your own server). The transcript text is then sent to your summary provider (Groq, Anthropic or Google). re:cap has no servers and never receives your data.

On Google Meet, re:cap posts a notice in the call chat so everyone knows. Elsewhere, tell everyone yourself.

Tell everyone on the call you are recording. A notice is posted in the Meet chat (you can turn that off in Settings), and a red badge shows on your screen. On Meet, recording stops by itself when the call ends.

Your microphone isn't allowed yet, so only other participants will be recorded. Allow microphone
