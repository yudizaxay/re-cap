    re:cap

    Record your calls on Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Zoom and more. Get the transcript, the notes and the action items.

    1

    Open your meeting

    Google Meet, Teams or Zoom in a browser tab. Any meeting site works.

    2

    Click the re:cap icon

    Choose this tab or your whole screen, then press Start. On Google Meet it stops by itself when the call ends.

    3

    Get your recap

    The recording, transcript and summary open here when the call ends.

    Free for life

    No account, no subscription, no ads. Just bring your own free API key.

    Stays on your machine

    Recordings are stored in your browser. re:cap has no servers to leak from.

    Import & export

    Back up every meeting as plain files, and bring them back any time.

    Download anything

    Save the video, the transcript or the notes from the ⋯ menu of any meeting.

    Video + audio

    Record the tab or your whole screen, along with your microphone.

    Transcript & AI notes

    Summary, decisions and action items, with timestamps that jump the video.
