Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. re:cap has no subscription and no ads. You use your own API key, and Groq offers one for free without a credit card.

Which meeting apps work?

Google Meet has the most support, including auto-stop and the chat notice. Teams, Zoom (web) and any other site open in a browser tab can be recorded too, and you press Stop yourself.

Do the other people know they are recorded?

On Google Meet, re:cap posts a notice in the call chat from your account. On other sites, please tell participants yourself. You are responsible for recording only where you have permission.

Where are my recordings stored?

In your browser's storage on your computer. Clearing browser data or removing the extension deletes them, so use Export to keep a copy.

Does it work with the Zoom or Teams desktop app?

Whole-screen mode records the picture, but on macOS the other side's voice is not captured. Browser tabs carry call audio, so use the web version for best results.
